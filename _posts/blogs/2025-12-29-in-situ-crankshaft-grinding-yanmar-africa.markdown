### Our In Situ Crankshaft Grinding Process

1. **Inspection** – Examine crankpin and main journals for wear, ovality, and runout.  
2. **Setup & Alignment** – Align portable grinding machine precisely to the crankshaft.  
3. **Precision Grinding** – Restore journal dimensions, taper, and roundness per OEM specifications.  
4. **Polishing** – Achieve correct surface finish for optimal bearing performance.  
5. **Post-Repair Verification** – Hardness testing and dimensional checks ensure compliance.  
6. **Reporting** – Full documentation with maintenance recommendations, metal stitching advice, and future inspection schedules.  

All procedures follow **Yanmar OEM standards and international quality protocols**.
