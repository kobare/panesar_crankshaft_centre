### Our In Situ Crankshaft Grinding Process

1. **Initial Inspection** – Journal wear, runout, ovality, and bearing condition are carefully measured.  
2. **Setup & Alignment** – Portable grinding machine is precisely aligned with the crankshaft in-situ.  
3. **Precision Grinding** – Journals are ground to restore diameter, roundness, and taper per OEM specs.  
4. **Final Polishing** – Surface finish is corrected to meet Ra values for bearing compatibility.  
5. **Post-Repair Testing** – Hardness checks and dimensional verification ensure compliance.  
6. **Reporting** – Comprehensive service report with recommendations for maintenance, metal stitching, or future inspections.  

All work follows **Wartsila technical standards and international quality protocols**, suitable for high-load marine and industrial engines.
