### **What is Thermal Cold Spray Welding?**

Thermal cold spray welding is a surface restoration process in which metallic powder particles are accelerated at high velocity onto a prepared surface. Unlike conventional welding, the material bonds without melting the base metal, minimizing heat-affected zones and preserving the component's original properties.

This process is ideal for restoring worn or damaged parts while reducing the risk of distortion, cracking, or changes in material structure.
