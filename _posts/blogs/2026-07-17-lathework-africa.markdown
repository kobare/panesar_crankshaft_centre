### **What is Lathework?**

Lathework is a precision machining process where a workpiece rotates while cutting tools remove material to achieve the required dimensions, surface finish, and tolerances. It is widely used for manufacturing new components as well as restoring worn or damaged machinery parts.

Precision lathework ensures that critical components operate smoothly, maintain proper alignment, and meet OEM specifications.
