### Our In Situ Crankshaft Grinding Process

1. **Initial Inspection** – Assess crankpin and main journals for wear, ovality, and runout.  
2. **Setup & Alignment** – Align portable grinding machine precisely to the crankshaft.  
3. **Precision Grinding** – Restore journal dimensions, roundness, and taper according to OEM specifications.  
4. **Polishing** – Achieve the required surface finish for optimal bearing performance.  
5. **Post-Repair Verification** – Hardness testing and dimensional verification to ensure compliance.  
6. **Reporting** – Comprehensive service report including recommendations for maintenance, metal stitching, and future inspections.  

All procedures follow **MAN OEM standards and international quality protocols**.
