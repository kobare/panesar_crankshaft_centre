### Our In Situ Crankshaft Grinding Process

1. **Inspection** – Assess wear, ovality, taper, and runout on main and crankpin journals.  
2. **Setup & Alignment** – Position portable grinding equipment with precise alignment.  
3. **Precision Grinding** – Restore journal geometry, diameter, and roundness.  
4. **Polishing** – Achieve optimal surface finish for bearing performance.  
5. **Verification** – Hardness testing and dimensional checks to confirm OEM compliance.  
6. **Reporting** – Detailed service report with maintenance recommendations and options for metal stitching or regrinding.  

All procedures adhere to **Caterpillar technical standards and international quality protocols**.
