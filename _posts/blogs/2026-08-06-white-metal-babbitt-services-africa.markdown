### **What are White Metal Babbitt Bearings?**

White metal bearings consist of a soft metal alloy lining bonded to a steel, cast iron, or bronze shell. The Babbitt alloy typically contains tin, lead, antimony, and copper, formulated to provide:

- Excellent embeddability to absorb foreign particles
- Good conformability to accommodate slight misalignment
- High seizure resistance under boundary lubrication conditions
- Low friction coefficient for efficient operation
- Good thermal conductivity for heat dissipation

Over time, Babbitt bearings can suffer from wear, fatigue cracking, bond failure, corrosion, and wiping due to oil starvation or overloading.
