### **The Role of Engine Bearings**

Engine bearings serve several vital functions:

- Support the crankshaft and connecting rods during rotation
- Maintain proper oil clearance for lubrication
- Absorb and distribute combustion forces
- Reduce friction between moving components
- Protect the crankshaft journals from wear

When bearings wear, seize, or fail, the consequences can be severe — including crankshaft damage, connecting rod failure, and complete engine destruction.

### **Signs Your Engine Bearings Need Replacement**

Common indicators of worn or failing engine bearings include:

- Low oil pressure readings
- Metallic knocking or rumbling sounds from the engine
- Excessive vibration during operation
- Metal particles or debris in the engine oil
- Crankshaft scoring or journal damage
- Uneven wear patterns on bearing surfaces
- Overheating in bearing areas

Early detection and prompt bearing replacement can prevent expensive secondary damage to the crankshaft and engine block.
