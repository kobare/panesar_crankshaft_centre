### **What is Connecting Rod Refurbishing?**

Connecting rod refurbishing is the process of restoring worn, damaged, or distorted connecting rods to OEM specifications. Over time, connecting rods can suffer from:

- Big-end bore distortion
- Small-end bushing wear
- Cracks from fatigue or overloading
- Bending or twisting from hydraulic lock or detonation
- Thread damage on rod bolts
- Fretting and surface wear at mating surfaces

Refurbishing involves precision machining, crack detection, straightening, bushing replacement, and resizing to restore the connecting rod to factory specifications.

### **Signs Your Connecting Rods Need Attention**

Common indicators of connecting rod problems include:

- Knocking or tapping sounds from the engine
- Low oil pressure
- Excessive vibration
- Metal particles in the engine oil
- Uneven piston or cylinder wear
- Engine misfire or loss of power
- Visible damage during engine teardown

Prompt connecting rod inspection and refurbishment can prevent severe engine damage and costly downtime.
