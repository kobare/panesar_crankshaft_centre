### **What is Line Boring?**

Line boring is a machining process used to restore the alignment and dimensions of worn or damaged bores. A boring bar is passed through the component, allowing material to be removed accurately and creating a precise bore that meets required specifications.

This process is commonly used where accurate alignment between multiple bearing points is essential for proper equipment operation.

### **Why Line Boring is Important**

Worn or misaligned bores can cause:

- Excessive movement between components
- Increased vibration and stress
- Accelerated wear of pins and bearings
- Reduced equipment performance
- Premature machinery failure

Precision line boring restores proper fit and alignment, improving reliability and extending the service life of expensive equipment.
