### **What is Cylinder Head Refacing?**

Cylinder head refacing is the precision machining process of resurfacing the cylinder head's mating surface to restore flatness and the correct surface finish. Over time, cylinder heads can develop problems including:

- Warping from overheating
- Corrosion and pitting from coolant leaks
- Surface erosion from combustion gases
- Indentations from failed head gaskets
- Uneven wear from improper torque sequences

Refacing removes a minimal amount of material to restore a perfectly flat, smooth surface that ensures proper head gasket sealing and even clamping force distribution.

### **Signs Your Cylinder Head Needs Refacing**

Common indicators of cylinder head surface problems include:

- Coolant in the engine oil
- White smoke from the exhaust
- Engine overheating
- Loss of compression
- Bubbles in the radiator or coolant reservoir
- External coolant or oil leaks
- Failed head gaskets
- Uneven cylinder compression readings

Prompt cylinder head inspection and refacing can prevent repeated gasket failures and more serious engine damage.
