### **What is Engine Block Resleeving?**

Engine block resleeving is the process of replacing worn, cracked, or scored cylinder liners (sleeves) within an engine block. Over time, cylinder walls can become damaged due to wear, overheating, poor lubrication, or foreign object damage.

Resleeving involves:

- Removing the old or damaged cylinder sleeve
- Precision machining of the cylinder bore
- Installing a new sleeve to OEM specifications
- Final honing to achieve the correct surface finish and tolerances

This process restores the cylinder to its original dimensions, ensuring proper piston fit, compression, and engine performance.

### **What is Line Boring?**

Line boring is a precision machining process used to restore the main bearing bore alignment in engine blocks. The main bearing bores must be perfectly aligned to support the crankshaft and ensure smooth engine operation.

Line boring involves:

- Precision machining of the main bearing housing bores
- Restoring proper alignment and concentricity
- Ensuring correct bearing crush and oil clearance
- Achieving OEM specifications for crankshaft fitment

Line boring is essential after engine block damage, overheating, or when bearing bores become distorted over time.
