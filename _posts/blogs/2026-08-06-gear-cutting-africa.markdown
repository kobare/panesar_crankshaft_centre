### **What is Gear Cutting?**

Gear cutting is the precision machining process of creating gear teeth on a workpiece to produce spur gears, helical gears, worm gears, bevel gears, and other gear types. This process requires specialized equipment and expertise to achieve the correct tooth profile, pitch, pressure angle, and surface finish.

Gear cutting can be performed to manufacture new gears from stock material or to replace worn or damaged gears in existing machinery.
